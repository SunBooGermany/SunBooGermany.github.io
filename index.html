    Research

    Research agenda

    My work sits at the interface of process systems engineering, operations research, and machine learning. The common objective is to design decision-making methods that remain computationally tractable while respecting feasibility under uncertainty.

    01

    Clean-energy system planning

    Long-horizon design and operation of green hydrogen, green ammonia, microgrid, offshore wind, and DAC-integrated systems under uncertain demand, renewable supply, cost, and policy conditions.

    02

    Stochastic optimization and decomposition

    Multi-period and multi-timescale planning models that connect strategic capacity decisions with operational recourse, with emphasis on feasibility-critical decision making.

    03

    Learning-augmented optimization

    Integration of reinforcement learning, Bayesian optimization, attention models, and optimization-compatible neural surrogates for scalable planning and control under uncertainty.
